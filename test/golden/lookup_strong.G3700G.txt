G3700  ὀπτάνομαι  optanomai  [G:V]  «to appear»
ὀπτάνω
 [in LXX: 3Ki.8:8 (רָאָה ni.), Tob.12:19 * ;]
late present as from ὤφθην (= ὁράω); mid., ὀπτάνομαι, to allow oneself to be seen, to appear. with dative, Act.1:3. (For exx. from π., see Deiss., LAE, 79, 2526; MM, ii, xviii.) †
